Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect venting and draft, confirm adequate combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, verify expansion tanks are charged correctly, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms.

After-Hours Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

When a commercial water heater fails, the clock starts immediately. Dish rooms stall, salon chairs empty, guests call the front desk, and tenants start complaining. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling quickly for emergency repairs throughout Cambridge and the surrounding area, and scheduled work can be done after hours to limit downtime.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: our technicians handle all of it on tank, tankless, and boiler-fed systems. The priority is pinpointing the fault and getting hot water back as quickly as possible, often by isolating the failed heater so the others carry the load. After that, we set up a maintenance schedule so it doesn't happen again, and every response is fully documented for your facility files.
